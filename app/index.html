About this guide

Names, descriptions and grades are the official ones, as PlayStation publishes them, cross-checked against the Exophase trophy list. Every guide is written in our own words, and every specific step or location carries a named source beside it. Where the guides disagree — or where a trophy's method is not yet confirmed — the guide says so instead of guessing. Five trophies were still unconfirmed in the guides at the time of writing: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin, plus the unique animal for Fair Game.

Progress is stored only in your browser (localStorage). Export downloads a JSON file you can keep or move to another device; Import reads it back. Nothing here sends any data anywhere — the page works fully offline.

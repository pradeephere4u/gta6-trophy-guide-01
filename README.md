Leonida Trophy Atlas — a complete, cited guide and offline tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI. Every trophy carries its official name, description and grade, a guide written in our own words with named sources beside each step, a three-stage roadmap to the platinum (about 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies), a missable-trophy flag for the one possibly missable step, and a checklist that saves in your browser with search, filters by story, missable, collectibles, activities and online, a spoiler mode that hides hidden trophy names until earned, and export/import of your progress. It works fully offline — no network requests at runtime.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.

No build step, no dependencies. The app is plain static files under `app/` — open `app/index.html` directly from a local server. The trophy data lives in `app/data/trophies.json`.

## What's inside

- **All 55 trophies** — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden, 1 missable. Official names, descriptions and grades as PlayStation publishes them, cross-checked against the Exophase trophy list.
- **A guide for every trophy** — written in our own words: what to do, where and how, and when it can be missed. Every specific step or location carries a named, linked source (PowerPyx, GTABase, Push Square, KeenGamer, EGamers, Exophase). Where the guides disagree or a method is not yet confirmed, the guide says so instead of guessing.
- **Roadmap to the Platinum** — Stage 1: play the story (60–80 hours, including the one possibly missable step in Chapter 1). Stage 2: reach 100% completion (about 100 hours). Stage 3: Strangers & cleanup (10–20 hours).
- **Tracker** — tick trophies off as you earn them; progress is saved in your browser only. Search, filter by kind and grade, hide story spoilers until earned, export your progress to a JSON file and import it back on another device.

## Sources

Guides were researched from PowerPyx, GTABase, Push Square, KeenGamer, EGamers, Games.gg, Insider Gaming and the Exophase trophy list. Each trophy links its sources in the app. No game art, logos, box art or screenshots are used anywhere in this guide.
